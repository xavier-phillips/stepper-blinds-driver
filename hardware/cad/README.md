# CAD Files

## Editable source

The editable mechanical model remains in Onshape: [assembly](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/d6564a825545e86e8dcb608a/w/c5c19c300cbe94424be6aa39/e/ca30e980ba381ea447399439).

The main mechanical component is the mount, featuring two compartments. A thick-walled compartment is load-bearing and contains the NEMA 17, alongside a thin-walled circuit compartment.

A face plate connects the NEMA 17 to the mount and guides the beaded chain. A friction-fit cosmetic lid hides the circuit and uses two pins for alignment and security. The sprocket is parametrically designed. The box has hexagonal ventilation holes to balance structural strength and airflow.

## Generated print files

Local exports are retained in `print-files/` outside the tracked source. STL files are mesh exports; BGCode files are printer jobs. The dated local archive contains earlier iterations. The editable model is the Onshape assembly above.
